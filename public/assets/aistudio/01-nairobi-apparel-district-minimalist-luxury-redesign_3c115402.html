The Heart of Art and Upcycling

Nairobi Apparel District is a Kenyan streetwear brand known as “The Heart of Art and Upcycling.” We emphasize art, upcycling, recycling, and the use of sustainable fabrics.

We take pride in our exclusive Kenyan designs and customer-tailored products, while also committing to reducing the environmental impact of the fast fashion industry.

Our vision is to be a leading voice in advocacy, cultural influence, and awareness creation through art and fashion. Each piece is a canvas for sustainable storytelling.
